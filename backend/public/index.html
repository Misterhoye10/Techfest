FAQ

Straight answers. No score guarantees.

What is Reca?

Reca is a study companion for WAEC and JAMB. You learn and practise on the web. Reca remembers what you missed and messages you on WhatsApp to check again.

Does Reca work on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is how Reca comes back to you. Link your number after you start so unprompted recall can reach you. The web app is where you do deeper study.

Is this official WAEC or JAMB?

No. Reca is exam style practice and explanations aligned to how students prepare. We do not claim official scanned papers unless we have a licensed bank.

How is Reca different from a chatbot?

A chatbot explains when you ask. Reca also tests you, stores what you got wrong, and comes back later without you opening an app. Memory over time is the product.

Do I need to pay?

Free is ₦0 forever for core revision. Plus is ₦1,500/mo, free during early access, then billed after pilot. Pro starts from ₦3,500/mo on the waitlist. We confirm before charging anyone.

Can my school use Reca?

Yes. Start students on Reca first. Cohort tools for teachers come after we prove the memory loop with real SS3 and JAMB candidates.
