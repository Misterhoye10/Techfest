    Reca is an AI-powered WAEC & JAMB study companion. Ask in plain language. Get AI explanations and short quizzes, then come back to what you missed on web and WhatsApp.

    • Learn: AI explains any syllabus topic in exam language.
    • Revise: WAEC- or JAMB-style practice, one question at a time. Or snap a past question.
    • Progress: AI memory tracks weak topics and when to revisit.
    • Uni updates: AI reads official admission pages and drops POST-UTME, CAPS, and deadline reminders for your target school.

    Four steps. AI on web and WhatsApp.

    1. Ask Open Learn and type a question. Reca’s AI explains in exam language.
    2. Quiz Tap Try 2 quick questions for an AI-graded check, or open Revise for eight.
    3. Track misses AI memory saves wrong answers in Progress for another look.
    4. Come back first Reca nudges you on web (popup) and WhatsApp when linked: study briefings, missed questions, and university updates.
    5. University updates Set your primary university under Settings → Exam & university. Reca summarizes official POST-UTME / CAPS / admission news with a source link. Always verify on the portal.

    Try this in under two minutes.

    1. Guest From signup, tap Continue as guest. No code needed.
    2. Learn Ask Explain photosynthesis simply.
    3. Quiz Tap Try 2 quick questions and miss one on purpose.
    4. Progress Open Progress to see what Reca remembered.
    5. Nudge Settings → Exam & university → Send a test nudge for the web popup (WhatsApp when linked).

    What Reca stores, plain and honest.

    • Your phone number is your account ID when you sign in.
    • Name, exam date, and theme stay in this browser until you sign out.
    • Quiz attempts and Progress sync to the record tied to that number.
    • Guest sessions are temporary until you link a number.

    Don’t paste school passwords or exam PINs into Reca.
