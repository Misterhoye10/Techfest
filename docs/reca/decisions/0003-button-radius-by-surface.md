# Decision 0003 — Button radius by surface

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Status:** Locked  
**Product:** Reca

## Decision

| Surface | Radius | Use |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Marketing (`index.html`, nav/hero/pricing CTAs) | **Full pill** (`999px`) | Friendly, Claude-scale consumer craft |
| Product (signup, chat, Learn/Revise forms) | **12px** | Calm tutor, matches fields/cards |

## Not for Reca

Government-style `0–4px` on marketing or student product. Reca is not an official portal.

## Consequence

Do not put full-pill CTAs inside the study app. Do not square-off marketing nav to look “official.”
