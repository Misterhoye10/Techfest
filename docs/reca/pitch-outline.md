# Reca — Pitch outline (judges)

**One line:** ChatGPT forgets. Reca doesn’t — it remembers what you got wrong and comes back.

**Track:** Case Study 2 · AI for Everyday Life · WhatsApp · free stack

---

## 60–90 second structure

1. **Problem (10s)** — WAEC/JAMB students reread notes; they don’t know what they’ve forgotten.  
2. **Gap (10s)** — Chatbots explain once. They don’t resurface *your* weak question later.  
3. **Product (10s)** — Reca on WhatsApp: explain → quiz → grade → remember → unprompted return.  
4. **Live demo (50s)** — Follow [demo-script.md](./demo-script.md). Hero = resurface.  
5. **Close (10s)** — Memory layer, not another explainer. Free stack. Ready to pilot.

---

## Slide titles (max 5)

1. The problem — forgetting after studying  
2. Why not ChatGPT — no persistent weak-topic memory  
3. Reca — WhatsApp + spaced resurfacing  
4. Live demo / loop diagram  
5. What’s next — pilot schools / more subjects  

---

## Numbers you can say (from product PRD)

- ~1.95M WASSCE candidates (2026 cycle cited in PRD)

## Do not say

- “We built a full learning platform / PDF / OCR”

**Say:** memory over time · WhatsApp habit · live resurface proof
