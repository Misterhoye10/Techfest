# Reca — Pitch outline (judges)

**One line:** Claude and ChatGPT remember when you come back. Reca comes to you first.

**Track:** Case Study 2 · AI for Everyday Life · personal WhatsApp habit · free stack

**Audience:** Out-of-school — student's own phone, after-hours study. Not an in-school deploy for this demo.

---

## Judge pushback: “Doesn’t Claude already have memory?”

**Say this (exact framing):**

> Claude and ChatGPT remember what you tell them when you come back and ask. Reca doesn’t wait for you to come back — it decides on its own when you’re due to forget something, and comes to you first.

**Distinction:** passive recall on request vs **active, scheduled, correctness-triggered follow-up** (plus a daily study briefing). Not “has memory” vs “no memory.”

---

## 60–90 second structure

1. **Problem (10s)** — WAEC/JAMB students reread notes; they don’t know what they’ve forgotten.  
2. **Gap (10s)** — Chatbots explain once. Their memory is passive — you have to come back and ask.  
3. **Product (10s)** — Reca on WhatsApp: explain → quiz → grade → remember → **unprompted** return + daily focus briefing.  
4. **Live demo (50s)** — Follow [demo-script.md](./demo-script.md). Hero = resurface (Reca messages first).  
5. **Close (10s)** — Active memory companion for personal study time. Free stack. Ready to pilot.
