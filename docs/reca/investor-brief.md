# Reca Investor Brief

RECA
Investor Brief
The memory layer for how Nigerian students actually study
AI BuildFest 2026, Track 3
1. The Problem
1,950,726 students sat the 2026 WASSCE across Nigeria, Benin Republic, Côte d'Ivoire and Equatorial Guinea, with only 61.54% scoring credits including English and Maths — a decline from the prior year. JAMB registered 2,243,816 candidates for the 2026 UTME, up over 10% from 2025. Millions of students are self-studying every year with no reliable way to know what they've actually forgotten.
Every major AI chatbot can explain a topic on request. None of them persistently remember that a specific student got a specific question wrong three days ago and proactively bring it back at the right moment. That gap — memory over time, not just answers in the moment — is the opportunity.
2. The Solution
Reca is a WhatsApp-native study companion. A student sends a topic or a photo of their notes, gets a plain-language explanation and instant practice questions, and gets graded in the same thread. Reca then remembers what they got wrong and messages them again — unprompted — days later to retest it. No app to download, no login, no dashboard. The channel is one Nigerian students already live in.
3. Why Now, Why WhatsApp
WhatsApp is the dominant messaging platform in Nigeria, already installed on the phones of students with limited data budgets.
Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API service-conversation pricing carries no monthly platform fee, keeping distribution cost near zero at student scale.
Exam-prep demand is calendar-fixed and recurring: WAEC and JAMB cycles repeat every year, giving a predictable acquisition window each academic season.

5. Business Model (Post-Hackathon Roadmap)
Freemium: free tier for individual students, funded by low marginal AI cost per interaction.
B2B2C: partner with private secondary schools and tutorial centres for a per-student or per-cohort subscription, with a teacher-facing progress view.
Sponsor-funded access: exam-prep sponsors, EdTech NGOs, or telcos offering zero-rated data bundles to subsidise free access for public-school students.
6. Traction Plan (Next 90 Days Post-Fest)
Pilot with 2–3 secondary schools or tutorial centres in Lagos/Ogun for a single WAEC prep cycle.
Expand syllabus coverage from 2 demo subjects to core WAEC subjects: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, English.
Introduce teacher/parent progress visibility as the first paid feature.
7. What Makes This Defensible
Data moat: accumulated per-student attempt history improves personalisation and retention over time — a new entrant starts from zero.
Distribution moat: already embedded in the channel students use daily, versus competitors requiring app downloads.
Domain moat: curriculum and question design tuned specifically to WAEC/JAMB, not generic global content.
8. The Ask
At AI BuildFest 2026, we're demonstrating a working end-to-end loop — capture, explain, test, grade, and unprompted resurfacing — built in two days on a fully free tool stack. We're seeking recognition and support to pilot Reca with real students for the next WAEC/JAMB cycle.
