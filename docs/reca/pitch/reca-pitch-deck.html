Solution

The solution

Study companion that returns on its own.

A student sends a topic or past-question photo. Reca explains in plain exam language, asks two quick questions, grades instantly — then resurfaces weak spots unprompted.

No new app. No dashboard homework. WhatsApp is already the habit.

AI solution

Use of AI

AI that teaches — then remembers.

Generation

Gemini explains WAEC/JAMB topics in plain language, builds 2 practice questions, grades answers, and reads past-question photos.

Memory decision

Wrong answers schedule a follow-up. Reca decides when to return — the student doesn't open the chat to dig it up.

Preset curriculum bank keeps the demo reliable offline. AI is the teacher; spaced recall is the product.
