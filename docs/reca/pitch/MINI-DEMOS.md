**Rubric hit:** Problem–Solution Fit · Creativity · Practical Usefulness  
**Proof:** Reca comes *first* (active memory), not ChatGPT-style wait.

| Time | Show | Say |
|------|------|-----|

| 0:48–0:55 | Cutaway | “A normal chatbot stops here.” |
| 0:55–1:20 | **Phone idle → Reca messages first** | Soft: “She didn’t ask. Reca came back.” |
| 1:20–1:35 | Reply correct answer → cleared | “That’s active memory.” |
| 1:35–2:00 | End card + live URL | “Built for everyday study. Reca comes first.” |

**Rubric hit:** User Experience · Execution · Practical Usefulness  
**Proof:** Clean Learn → Revise → Progress in one glance.

| Time | Show | Say |
|------|------|-----|
| 0:00–0:10 | Signup → **Continue as guest** | “Same product on web — no OTP needed.” |
