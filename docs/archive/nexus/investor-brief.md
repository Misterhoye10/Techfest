NEXUS
Investor Brief
The outside brain for everyday responsibilities
AI BuildFest 2026, Track 3
1. The Problem
Students, employees, entrepreneurs and caregivers manage several responsibilities at once, scattered across notebooks, calendars, chat threads and memory. Important activities get forgotten, deadlines get missed, and people spend real time and stress just deciding what to do next. Unlike a single-audience niche, this problem touches essentially every working adult and student in Nigeria — traders, employees, small business owners, and parents alike — every single day, not just during a season.
Every major AI chatbot can extract a task or answer a question when asked. None of them make capture effortless enough to become a daily habit, and none reliably resurface the right thing at the right time without the user first doing the organisational work themselves.
2. The Solution
Nexus lets a person say one messy, natural sentence — mixing a reminder, a meeting, a deadline, and a follow-up all together — and takes responsibility for the rest. It extracts each item, decides what kind of action it needs, holds it, and brings it back to the user exactly when it matters, unprompted. When plans change, the user says so once, and Nexus adjusts without any manual re-entry. The product promise: never carry your responsibilities in your head again.
3. Why Now
Large language models have only recently become reliable enough to extract multiple structured, distinct items from a single messy, conversational sentence — the core capability this product depends on.
Nigeria's informal and multi-income economy means most adults are already juggling several concurrent responsibilities — a job, a side hustle, family logistics — with no single system holding all of it.
A browser-based MVP has zero distribution friction — no app store approval, no download, works on any device with a browser.

5. Business Model (Post-Hackathon Roadmap)
Freemium: unlimited capture and resurfacing for individuals, funded by low marginal AI cost per interaction.
Team/business tier: small business owners and teams get shared visibility into business-related items — a natural upsell for traders and entrepreneurs managing suppliers, customers, and staff.
Potential channel partnerships: productivity and business-tooling platforms serving Nigerian SMEs as a distribution channel for the team tier.
6. Traction Plan (Next 90 Days Post-Fest)
Pilot with a small group of real users across the three core personas — a trader, a student, a working parent — to validate resurfacing timing and extraction accuracy in the wild.
Introduce voice-note capture, the most natural input method for on-the-go Nigerian users.
Build the first team/shared-visibility feature for small business use cases.
7. What Makes This Defensible
Data moat: accumulated capture history per user improves extraction accuracy and resurfacing timing over time.
Habit moat: once someone's mental ‘inbox’ lives in Nexus, switching cost is behavioural, not just technical — they'd have to rebuild the habit elsewhere.
Scope moat: purpose-built for effortless capture and proactive resurfacing, not a general-purpose chatbot with task features bolted on.

We're seeking recognition and support to pilot Nexus with real users across trading, student, and working-parent contexts.
